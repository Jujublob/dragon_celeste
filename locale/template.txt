### Guide Book ###
Celestial Dragon Guide=
Explains how to use the mod=
Close=
The Celestial Dragon is a rare boss that never attacks first. Defeat it to obtain celestial materials.=
Dragon scales are used to craft the Celestial Sword and the armour set.=
Celestial Pickaxe: Mines almost instantly, in a 3x3 area.=
Celestial Armour: A full set grants near-immunity and reflects part of the damage back to the attacker.=
Speed-Jump Crystal: Right-click to toggle speed and low gravity. It must then recharge.=
Star Flowers grow in grasslands and snowy biomes. Surround a diamond block with 8 of them to craft a Celestite Block. Place a dragon egg on it to hatch your own dragon.=
Taming: Right-click a dragon with a Speed-Jump Crystal to become its master. It will then follow you. Sneak and right-click with an empty hand to toggle follow or stay.=
Healing: Right-click a dragon with a Star Flower to restore part of its health (or with a Scale for full health).=
Riding: Right-click your dragon with an empty hand. Look in the direction you want to fly and press forward; jump to ascend, sneak to descend. Right-click again to dismount.=
