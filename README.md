# Celestial Dragon (`dragon_celeste`)

A Luanti game mod by **Jujublob** that adds a rare, powerful Celestial Dragon boss and celestial equipment.

## Features

* **Celestial Dragon** — a flying boss with 30,000–50,000 HP. It only retaliates when attacked and can be tamed, healed, and ridden.
* **Celestial Scales** — dropped by the dragon and used to craft celestial gear.
* **Celestial Sword** — configurable damage against creatures and dragons.
* **Celestial Pickaxe** — near-instant mining in a 3×3 area, with protection checks.
* **Celestial Armour** — a full set provides defence and reflects some melee damage.
* **Speed-Jump Crystal** — a timed speed and low-gravity boost with a cooldown.
* **Star Flowers** — glowing flowers that generate in grassland and snowy biomes, heal dragons, and are used to craft Celestite Blocks.
* **Dragon Egg** — place it on a Celestite Block to hatch a dragon.
* **Guide Book** — an in-game guide to the mod's mechanics.

## Progression

1. Find Star Flowers in grassland or snowy biomes.
2. Craft a Celestite Block from 8 Star Flowers and 1 Diamond Block.
3. Fight a wild dragon or hatch one from an egg placed on Celestite.
4. Use the scales it drops to craft celestial equipment.

## Dependencies

Required: `default`, `mobs` (Mobs Redo), and `3d_armor`.
Optional: `moreores`.

## Settings

Settings are available under *Settings → All settings → Mods → dragon_celeste*. They include natural spawning, spawn rarity, equipment stats, boost duration, and whether dragons attack monsters.

## Translations

English and French translation files are included in `locale/`. The English strings are the source strings; contributions for other languages are welcome.
